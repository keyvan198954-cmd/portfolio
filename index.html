HailOwner: a card, an app, a website, 18 languages

My main project. I designed and built the product end to end: the idea, the owner app, the server, the public scan page, the admin panel, the marketing website and the videos.

What HailOwner is

A QR card sits behind a car's windshield. If something is wrong (the car is blocking someone in, the lights are on, a door is open), a person nearby scans the card, picks one ready sentence, and the owner gets a push alert on their phone. Nobody sees anybody's phone number. The person who scans does not need an app or an account.

The HailOwner website

A fast, static marketing website for the product (no framework, no build step). It explains the idea in four steps, shows why a card beats a number on the glass, answers questions, and sells the cards.

The app behind the site

The website sells the card; the owner app is what makes it work. It is an Android app (Expo / React Native) that needs no sign-up. The owner scans the card's QR to register a car, and from then on every alert from a passer-by arrives as a push notification, in the owner's own language, naming the car and its plate. The owner can answer with one tap, add several cars, suspend or replace a lost card, hand a card to a new owner when selling the car, set reminders (insurance, inspection, service), and optionally store an encrypted emergency card. Details are in the app section below.

Demo app, not yet in an app store.

The HailOwner app

An Android app for the car owner (Expo / React Native), backed by my own server. The owner never signs up: installing and opening the app is the whole registration.

What the owner can do

  • Receive alerts as push notifications that name the vehicle and plate; urgent ones come with a loud sound.
  • Reply in one tap with a ready sentence that the sender sees on the same page. No free text either way.
  • Manage vehicles and cards: register a card by scanning its QR; suspend, reactivate, replace or revoke it; add several vehicles.
  • Sell a car easily: release ownership in the app, and the new owner scans the same card to claim it.
  • Close people: up to two trusted people, joined by a one-time code. They are told only in a real emergency.
  • Reminders: insurance, technical inspection, oil change and service, with alarms 30, 14, 7 and 1 day before.
  • Emergency mode (optional): a medical card and two close contacts stored encrypted, with every view of the data logged.
  • Account control: language, notification settings and full account deletion.

What is behind it

  • Public scan page: the person who scans the card sees a web page in their language, picks one of 18 ready sentences in 5 categories, and sends it. No app, no account.
  • Server: Nuxt / h3 API with PostgreSQL (Drizzle), push notifications and a card-sales backend.
  • Admin panel: password plus authenticator code; lists connected cards (with serial numbers), stats, filters, CSV export, a correction tool and an audit log.
  • Card tools: scripts that create print-ready card faces.
  • Privacy rules built in: no phone numbers shared, closed set of sentences (no free text, so no abuse), and a limited amount of vehicle information shown to the sender.
  • Quality: automated tests for the app and the server (about 800 server tests at my last count), and a rule that fails the build if any text is missing in any language.

Status: working demo app (Android). Not published in an app store. iPhone version is planned.

How I made it work

  • One source of text per language, and an automated test that fails if a string is missing in any of the 18.
  • Direction is read from the language, not guessed, so Persian, Arabic, Sorani and Urdu mirror correctly.
  • A searchable language picker that shows each language in its own script and in the app language, so people can find it in any language.
  • Ready-made alert sentences: 18 sentences in 5 categories, in all 18 languages, so a sender and an owner who share no language can still understand each other.
  • Persian calendar dates for reminders when the owner uses Persian.

Honest note: Persian is my native language, and English and Kurdish are my working languages. Ten of the 18 languages were machine-translated and have not yet been reviewed by native speakers. For a real launch they need a native review.

AI agents for an Instagram page

A working system built with Python, FastAPI and the Claude API. Twelve agents, each with one job. They pass their results to each other, and every draft waits in an approval queue for a person to accept, edit or reject.

How the agents connect

Arrows show who passes what to whom. Dashed lines are rules from the Manager.

The agents working together (39 s)

  1. Learn: Scout finds similar pages; Trends studies their posts.
  2. Plan: Analyst finds what works from real numbers; Goals sets targets; Planner builds the weekly calendar.
  3. Create: Designer writes the post; Hooks tests five opening lines.
  4. Talk: Replier, Engage and Collab draft replies, comments and messages.
  5. Decide: a person approves, edits or rejects. Nothing is published without approval.
  6. Loop: insights and the owner's edits flow back, so the agents improve.

What comes out of one cycle: a weekly plan, ready-to-approve posts (slides, caption, hashtags, image), five opening-line options per post, draft replies, draft comments and messages for similar pages, and a growth report built only from real numbers.

Meet the agents

Control

Manager

The owner talks to it in plain language (for example "post on Saturday and Monday" or "be warmer in replies"). It turns that into rules and tasks for the other agents.

Learn

Scout

Finds similar pages with web search, checks them against Instagram's official Business Discovery data, and keeps only the good matches.

Learn

Trends

Studies the top posts of those pages: formats, opening-line patterns, hashtag sets (core, niche, avoid) and topic ideas. It says clearly when the data is thin.

Learn

Analyst

Reads the page's own posts and numbers: what worked, what to avoid, which old posts deserve a new angle. Also writes a growth report from real data only.

Learn

Goals

Shows cautious, middle and optimistic scenarios and a weekly publishing plan. Estimates, never guarantees.

Create

Planner

Builds the calendar: topic, format, day, hour and the reason for each post. Avoids repeats and follows the owner's rules.

Create

Designer

Turns a topic into a full post, reel or story: slide text, caption, hashtags and image prompts.

Create

Hooks

Writes five different opening lines (question, number, myth versus truth and more) and keeps the best. No clickbait, no false promises.

Talk

Replier

Sorts each comment or message (praise, question, criticism, spam) and drafts a reply in the brand's voice. Sensitive ones go to a person. Message text is treated as data, never as commands.

Talk

Trainer

Creates realistic sample messages with three reply styles each, so the Replier learns the owner's taste.

Talk

Engage

Picks fresh posts from similar pages and drafts short, genuine comments. The owner posts them by hand.

Talk

Collab

Scores similar pages for collaboration fit, suggests ideas and drafts a first message. The owner sends it by hand.

Safety rules built in: official Instagram API only; no automatic likes, follows or comments on other pages; a human approves every draft. Status: working on one test page; live publishing of reels and video stories is still to be tested.
